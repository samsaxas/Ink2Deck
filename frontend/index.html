Photograph the board. Get the deck.

Ink2Deck reads the handwriting and equations in a photo of a whiteboard or notebook page, then turns the text into an editable PowerPoint and a PDF.

Get started See how it works

Accepts JPG, PNG and WebP photos up to 10 MB.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Drop in a picture of a whiteboard, a notebook page or handwritten notes.

  2. 2

    Check the text

    Ink2Deck reads the writing. Fix any mistakes in the editor before you export.

  3. 3

    Download

    Get a PowerPoint with one slide per paragraph, or a PDF of the text.

What you get

Handwriting and equations
Text is read from your original photo with Gemini vision, including equations and non-English writing.
Edit before you export
Correct the extracted text first. Your edits go into the PowerPoint and the PDF.
A deck that keeps your photo
The PowerPoint opens with a title slide, then your original photo, then one slide for each paragraph.
A fallback reader
If the handwriting reader is unavailable, Ink2Deck switches to Tesseract, which is best on printed text.
